# Phase 2 — HMM cross-validation & directedness (first pass)

Session `5c0c560e` (VISp, VISpm, SCm, MRN recorded together), 400 trials subsampled,
20 ms bins, 0.5 s pre-stimulus baseline. Generated by `notebooks/01_hmm_triplet.ipynb`.

## Cross-validated number of states K

Held-out (trial-split) log-likelihood, 3 folds, K ∈ {2..7}. **Best K = 2 for every
region.** The richer K=5 model used earlier was overfitting: a parsimonious
baseline + evoked description is what generalises at this trial count / bin size.

| region | best K | forward_fraction @ bestK | baseline_exit (ms) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| VISp  | 2 | 0.529 | — (baseline not dominant) |
| VISpm | 2 | 0.514 | — |
| SCm   | 2 | 0.771 | 210 |
| MRN   | 2 | 0.692 | — |

(`forward_fraction`: 0.5 = reversible mixing, →1 = directed metastable chain. Earlier at
K=5, with a shuffle null, all four were significantly > 0.5, p<0.001.)

## Reading it

- **Motor midbrain is more directed than visual cortex.** SCm (0.77) and MRN (0.69) show
  clearer directed state sequences than VISp (0.53) or VISpm (0.51) — consistent across
  K=2 and K=5. Slightly counter-intuitive (the sensory area is *less* sequential), and
  worth chasing.
- **K=2 is provisional.** CV here is coarse (250-trial subsample, 25 EM iters, 3 folds)
  and the test-LL differences between K can be small; inspect the CV curve
  (`results/figures/phase2_cv/cv_states.png`) rather than trusting the argmax, and re-run
  with `MAX_TRIALS=None` before drawing conclusions.
- **VISp/VISpm have no dominant baseline state** (so baseline-exit latency is `nan` by
  design) — expected for sparse, low-rate visual cortex (VISp ≈ 2.8 Hz; see the firing
  table). SCm leaves baseline at ~210 ms, after the ~170 ms median RT.

## Caveats / next

- One session, subsampled. Repeat on the replicate sessions and at full trial count.
- The interesting cross-area story (does SCm↔MRN exchange?) needs a **joint multi-region
  HMM/rSLDS** and state-occupancy cross-correlation, not per-region fits — Phase 3.
